Hiring me

What I am looking for

This page exists so you can decide in ninety seconds whether it is worth a conversation. If the answer is yes, my inbox is at the bottom.

The role

A senior or lead position where I own an AI platform or a generative AI product line end to end — architecture, the team, the roadmap, and the operational reality of running it afterwards. Titles that usually fit: Lead / Staff / Principal AI Engineer, AI Solution Architect, Head of AI Engineering.

I am most useful where the data is difficult and the stakes are physical: industrial, energy, manufacturing, mobility, or any regulated environment where an answer has to be defensible rather than merely plausible. I am least useful on consumer growth problems.

Location

Germany. I am relocating permanently and I am not exploring alternatives. Munich, Berlin, Erlangen, Hamburg, Stuttgart, Frankfurt, Nürnberg — on-site or hybrid, all fine.

The paperwork, answered upfront

QuestionAnswer
Work authorisation EU Blue Card eligible under the Skilled Immigration Act — recognised engineering degree, IT-professional route, salary threshold met. The accelerated skilled-worker procedure (§81a AufenthG) is available to an employer who wants to shorten it further.
How long until I can start Three months from a signed contract. My 90-day notice period runs in parallel with the visa process, and standard issuance is 4–6 weeks — so the paperwork completes inside the notice period, not after it.
Can you come to an interview Yes, at short notice and at no cost to you. I hold a current German-issued 2-year multi-entry Schengen visa. It is my third consecutive Schengen visa, after Spain and Austria.
Have you worked in Germany Four years of delivery with Siemens Energy Germany, including on-site work in Duisburg and, most recently, Berlin — where I attended DBPD PI Planning as Project Lead.
Language English C1, my working language for a decade. German A2 and actively studying toward B2.

What I bring that is hard to hire

  • Production generative AI — shipped, adopted, and operated, including on-call. Not a pilot.
  • Deep domain understanding of rotating equipment: turbines, compressors, condition monitoring, sensor time-series. Formal training from a manufacturer's own global training centre.
  • The polyglot reality of industrial software — Python alongside C#/.NET, C++ and Java teams, event schemas and latency budgets negotiated across them.
  • Leading distributed engineers across India, Germany and Spain in an Agile/Scrum and SAFe setup.
  • Governance instincts from a regulated enterprise: access control, audit trails, EU data residency, security gates in CI.

How I like to work

  • Production-first. If it cannot be deployed, monitored and rolled back, it is not finished.
  • I operate what I build. Ownership does not stop at the merge.
  • Written decisions over meetings. Design docs, postmortems, evaluation results.
  • Small teams with real ownership, over large teams with divided responsibility.

What I am not looking for

  • Pure research positions with no path to production.
  • Roles where "AI strategy" means slide decks and no system.
  • Anything requiring me to abandon the industrial domain I spent eleven years building.

Get in touch

A short note about the problem you are solving is enough. I reply to everything serious within 24 hours, including to say no.
